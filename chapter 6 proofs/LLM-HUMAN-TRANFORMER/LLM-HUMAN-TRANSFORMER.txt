The evaluation was conducted on a sample of 300 scraped roles. Human inspection identified 128 real internships, 9 ambiguous cases, and 163 fake or non-internship roles. ChatGPT5.5 produced the same final distribution as the human inspection, with 128 real internships, 9 ambiguous cases, and 163 fake roles. The Transformer classified 132 roles as real internships and 168 roles as fake or non-internships. Since the Transformer final output only separates roles into real or fake internship classes, it does not produce an ambiguous category. Compared with the human review on clear real/fake cases, the Transformer achieved 95.19% accuracy, 93.85% precision, and 95.31% recall. These results show that the Transformer is highly effective at detecting real internships from noisy scraped job data, while ambiguous cases still benefit from human or ChatGPT5.5 review.

Precision : Measures how trustworthy the system is when it says a role is a real internship. High precision means fewer fake roles are wrongly accepted as internships.

Recall : Measures how many real internships the system successfully finds. High recall means fewer real internships are missed.
